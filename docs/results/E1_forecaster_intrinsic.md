## Goal

Comparison set:
1. **No FC** (raw |defender_last - target|² in body+scaled frame)
2. **Naive const-vel** (math only, no learning) — completed
3. **τ-aware Flow** (Phase 3c, ams_v9_multi_v1) — completed

## Results

### Best ckpt + 100K val MSE (per forecaster)

| forecaster | best step | avg_mse | τ=10 | τ=25 | τ=50 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| τ-aware Flow (ams_v9_multi_v1) | 90000 | 0.0064 | 0.0025 | 0.0053 | 0.0115 |
| τ-unaware Flow seed 42 (main, tau_unaware_v1) | 99999 | 0.0058 | 0.0027 | 0.0043 | 0.0103 |
| τ-unaware Flow seed 0 | 95000 | 0.0069 | 0.0040 | 0.0060 | 0.0107 |
| τ-unaware Flow seed 1 | 70000 | 0.0080 | 0.0051 | 0.0081 | 0.0108 |
| τ-unaware Flow **3-seed avg** | — | **0.0069** ± 0.0009 | 0.0039 ± 0.0010 | 0.0061 ± 0.0016 | 0.0106 ± 0.0002 |
| **τ-unaware Det** ⚡ | **35000** | **0.0053** | 0.0030 | 0.0039 | **0.0089** |

### 🔥 Critical findings (multi-seed 학습 후)
1. **Det이 모든 Flow보다 intrinsic MSE 더 낮음** (avg 0.0053 vs Flow seed 42 best 0.0058 vs Flow 3-seed avg 0.0069). Det best step 35K → 빨리 수렴 후 plateau (Flow는 100K 끝까지 개선).
2. **Flow seed variance 38% spread** (0.0058 ~ 0.0080). Det은 single seed이지만 가장 안정.
3. **τ=50 long-delay에서 Det 0.0089** (Flow 0.0103-0.0108) — single-step prediction에서 Det이 일관 우월.
4. **Flow의 differentiating value = uncertainty calibration** (D4): Det은 deterministic point estimate라 uncertainty 표현 X. Paper main contribution은 D4 + E2 deployment performance에 의존.

### Diagnostic 결과 (D1 — apples-to-apples, n=512)
| τ | τ-aware last MSE | τ-unaware MSE | **ratio (unaware / aware)** |
|---|---|---|---|
| 10 | 0.0008 | 0.0025 | 2.98× worse |
| 25 | 0.0045 | 0.0051 | 1.13× |
| 50 | 0.0106 | 0.0100 | **0.94× better** ⚡ |

### Diagnostic D2 — Shuffled defender_last (model 사용 검증)
| τ | normal | shuffled | ratio |
|---|---|---|---|
| 10 | 0.0026 | 0.5608 | **218×** worse → model이 defender_last 적극 사용 ✓ |
| 25 | 0.0052 | 0.5441 | **105×** |
| 50 | 0.0105 | 0.5756 | **55×** |

### Diagnostic D4 — Variance (10 Euler runs std per dim)
| τ | mean_std | pos std (avg 3 dim) | vel std (avg 3 dim) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 10 | 0.0171 | 0.008 | 0.026 |
| 25 | 0.0226 | 0.010 | 0.038 |
| 50 | 0.0270 | 0.010 | 0.047 |

→ vel uncertainty monotone ↑ (long-delay calibration ✓)

### Naive const-vel intrinsic (n=512)
| τ | naive MSE | identity MSE | improvement (ID − naive) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 10 | 0.0031 | 0.0025 | **−0.0005** (naive worse) |
| 25 | 0.0201 | 0.0164 | **−0.0037** (naive worse) |
| 50 | 0.0676 | 0.0509 | **−0.0167** (naive worse) |

→ **Naive const-vel 이 identity baseline (defender_last 그대로 쓰기)보다 worse** — defender direction change가 잦아 const-vel 가정 깨짐. **Trained forecaster의 학습 가치 명확** (paper 강한 finding).

## Devil's Advocate

### 짚어야 할 약점
1. **τ-aware history length 차이** (D1): τ-aware가 τ+1 step history vs τ-unaware K=50 step. τ=10에서 τ-aware는 11 step, τ-unaware는 50 step → unfair (τ-aware가 적은 정보로 더 정확). 단 τ=50 동등 → fair comparison 영역.
2. **τ-unaware τ=1 boundary**: model_mse 0.0025 > raw|defender_last - target|² 0.0001 → identity 학습 안 함. τ=1 sample 빈도 1/50 (uniform) → under-trained.
3. **Single forecaster training seed for τ-aware**: τ-aware는 single seed (ams_v9_multi_v1). 비교 multi-seed 약함. Limitation 명시.
4. **Naive const-vel underperforms identity** — interesting finding but also implies "test-time choice of baseline matters". Paper에서 identity baseline도 명시 권장.

### Reviewer 예상 공격 + 답
- **"τ-aware가 τ=10에서 훨씬 좋은데 왜 τ-unaware?"** → "Deployment realism: instantaneous τ measurement infrastructure unavailable in cellular/SATCOM scenarios. τ-unaware achieves competitive accuracy (1.13× at τ=25, 0.94× at τ=50) without this requirement."
- **"flow matching vs deterministic 차이는?"** → row #6 deterministic ablation 결과 (TBD 학습 끝나야)
